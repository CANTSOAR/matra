**Between** [STEWARD LEGAL NAME] ("**Matra**", currently Aryan Malik as interim steward until a non-profit or fiscal sponsor takes over, at which point this agreement transfers to it) **and** [ADVISOR NAME] ("**Advisor**"). **Effective date:** [DATE].

## 1. What Matra is

Matra is open-source, non-profit software for survivors of violence ([GOVERNANCE.md](../../GOVERNANCE.md)). It has no business model, no equity and no revenue to share. Matra has not been audited and is not used with real survivor data.

## 2. The role

Advisor will give informal, non-binding advice on [AREA: e.g. evidence and privilege / trauma-informed interviewing / survivor advocacy and pilot design], for up to [about 2] hours per month. Typically: review documents or prototypes Matra sends, and join up to [one] [45]-minute call per quarter.

Term: [12] months from the effective date. Either side may end this at any time by written notice (email is enough), no reason needed.

## 3. No payment, no equity

This role is unpaid. Advisor receives no salary, equity, options, revenue share, fee, or other compensation. Matra may reimburse pre-approved expenses and, if grant funds allow, offer an honorarium of [AMOUNT] under a written addendum, so long as the honorarium is permitted by Advisor's employer and does not depend on any funder, case, referral or product decision.

## 4. Advice only. No authority

- Advisor is a volunteer adviser, not an employee, officer, director, agent, partner or attorney of Matra, and cannot bind or speak for Matra.
- Advisor and Matra are not in an attorney-client, therapist-client or other professional relationship. **Advice given is Advisor's general view, not legal, clinical or professional advice to Matra or anyone else, and Matra will get its own licensed counsel for decisions.** Matra decides what to do with the advice and is responsible for its own decisions.
- Advisor will not be asked to, and should not, represent or advise survivors through Matra under this agreement.

## 5. Conflicts of interest

Advisor will disclose in writing any financial or professional interest that could affect their advice, including: a role with a funder, a law firm or legal-aid office that might join the platform, a competing product, or a party adverse to Matra's partners. Advisor will recuse from questions where such an interest exists. Matra will not ask Advisor to approve anything in which Matra itself has a financial interest (it has none). Advisors who are lawyers keep full responsibility for their own professional conflicts rules, including any rule about advising a service that may later list or route to lawyers.

## 6. Confidentiality (narrow)

The code, design and plans are open source and **not confidential**. Advisor may discuss them.

Advisor must keep confidential only:

1. **Survivor information.** Matra will not give Advisor any real survivor data. If Advisor encounters any by accident, they will stop, not copy it, and tell Matra.
2. **Unpatched security vulnerabilities** that Matra shares in confidence, until they are fixed or 90 days pass.
3. **Non-public information about a partner organization** (for example, a clinic's internal operations) that the partner gave in confidence.

This section doesn't restrict disclosure required by law (including mandatory-reporting duties and compulsory process), and Advisor will tell Matra first where lawful. It also doesn't restrict Advisor from reporting suspected wrongdoing to authorities, or from speaking about their own professional views.

## 7. Intellectual property

- Advice and feedback are given freely. Matra may use them without attribution or payment.
- Advisor keeps ownership of their prior work. Advisor grants Matra a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use any materials they voluntarily supply (for example, a comment letter or question wording) in the project under the project's license ([AGPL-3.0-only](../../LICENSE) for code, [CC BY 4.0] for documents unless they say otherwise in writing).
- If Advisor submits code, they do so under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Advisor will not submit anything they don't have the right to share, including material belonging to their employer, client files or confidential records.

## 8. Public listing and endorsement

- Matra may list Advisor's name, title and organization on an "Advisory Council" page **only after** Advisor has agreed in writing to the specific wording, and only for **[specific activities, e.g. 'reviewed the intake question set']**. Listing doesn't mean Advisor, or their organization, endorses Matra, certifies it as safe or legally compliant, or approved any specific feature.
- Advisor may withdraw consent for listing at any time. Matra will remove it within [10] business days and won't use earlier printed or archived materials after the next reprint.
- Matra won't use the name or logo of Advisor's institution without that institution's separate written permission.
- Advisor will not claim to speak for Matra.

## 9. No guarantee, no indemnity, limited liability

Matra provides no warranty about the project. Advisor gives advice "as is" and in good faith. **Advisor is not liable to Matra or anyone else for good-faith advice**, and Matra will not hold Advisor responsible for what Matra builds or decides. [COUNSEL: add indemnification by Matra or the fiscal sponsor for Advisor's good-faith volunteer activity, and confirm the sponsor's D&O or volunteer liability coverage extends to advisors. Advisors will ask for this.] Nothing here limits liability that can't legally be limited.

## 10. Safety and ethics

Advisor and Matra will treat survivors' safety and privacy as the priority. Matra will not ask Advisor to help market, fundraise from or recruit survivors, and no one will ask a survivor to share their story for promotional use through this arrangement.

This agreement doesn't assign or transfer rights except that Matra may assign it to a successor non-profit steward that keeps the commitments in GOVERNANCE.md, with notice to Advisor.
