# Attorney Partner Terms (template)

> **Template. Not reviewed by a lawyer.** Governs attorneys and legal aid offices that appear in the directory and receive records survivors choose to share. **Counsel must first decide whether Matra's attorney list is a (a) directory, (b) nonprofit lawyer referral service, or (c) legal service plan under NJ RPC 7.3(e), and whether registration is needed** ([../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md](../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md) sections 5 and 9). Do not launch the directory until that is answered, and do not run it from a for-profit entity.
>
> **Relationship to the MOU.** This is the short standard-terms version for an individual directory attorney. Legal-aid organizations, clinics and firms should use the fuller [Attorney Partner MOU](../ATTORNEY_PARTNER_MOU_TEMPLATE.md). Where the two differ, the MOU controls; keep them consistent when either changes.

**Between** [STEWARD LEGAL NAME] ("**Matra**") **and** [ATTORNEY / FIRM / LEGAL AID OFFICE] ("**Attorney**"). **Effective date:** [DATE].

## 1. What this is, and isn't

Matra lists vetted attorneys. A survivor decides whether to share her records with one of them. **Matra doesn't assign cases, rank attorneys, recommend a particular attorney, give legal advice, or take part in the attorney-client relationship.** Any representation is solely between the survivor and Attorney, under a separate engagement. Matra never directs Attorney's professional judgment (RPC 5.4(c)).

## 2. No money between us

- Attorney pays nothing to be listed, and Matra pays nothing to Attorney.
- Matra receives **no** referral fee, fee share, percentage, subscription, per-lead payment, or other consideration from Attorney, a funder, or anyone else that depends on a survivor, a referral or a case. (RPC 5.4(a), 7.2(c), 7.3(d); ACPE 732 / CAA 44 / UPL 54.)
- Fees for the survivor's matter are between her and Attorney, and may be paid by the survivor, a legal-aid funder, or a state victim-compensation program. Matra has no claim on any of it. [COUNSEL: confirm whether RPC 5.4(a)(5) allows the nonprofit to receive court-awarded fees, and recommend that this project take none.]
- Matra will not tell survivors that victim-compensation payments make an attorney's work "pro bono", since they are not.

## 3. Vetting

Attorney must provide, and keep current: full name, bar admission(s) and number(s), good standing, practice areas, and organization. Matra verifies the bar record and contacts the organization before listing, and records who verified and when. Re-verification happens at least every [12] months. Matra may remove a listing if Attorney's license is suspended, if a survivor or partner makes a credible complaint, or if Attorney breaches these terms. Matra will tell Attorney why, where safe to do so.

Attorney won't allow anyone else to use their account, and must tell Matra if their device or credentials are compromised.

## 4. Attorney's duties on receiving records

Records shared through Matra reach Attorney only when the survivor chooses. When Attorney receives one:

1. **Confidentiality.** Attorney treats it as confidential under RPC 1.6, and as information of a prospective client under RPC 1.18 from the moment it is received, even if Attorney declines the matter.
2. **Prompt response.** Attorney will tell the survivor within [3] business days (the MOU target) whether they can take her matter, say when they can't, and refer her onward where appropriate. Silence is not acceptance.
3. **Conflicts.** Check conflicts on receipt. If Attorney can't act, decline in the platform, delete any downloaded copy within [10] days unless a legal hold applies (MOU §6.5), and tell the survivor.
4. **Key hygiene.** Keep the private key and passphrase secure. Rotate if lost or compromised. Use only devices Attorney controls.
5. **No onward sharing** without the survivor's informed consent, except as the law requires. Attorney will tell her if they are compelled to disclose.
6. **Minors and mandatory reporting.** Attorney is responsible for their own reporting duties (including N.J.S.A. 9:6-8.10). [COUNSEL: reconcile with RPC 1.6 for historical abuse disclosures.]
7. **No marketing use.** Records, content and the fact of a survivor's use of Matra won't be used for marketing, fundraising or publicity, or as a lead for other services.

## 5. Survivor's rights

The survivor can revoke a share and delete her records. Attorney acknowledges that revocation stops future access and doesn't undo copies Attorney already lawfully holds, and agrees to handle those under section 4.

## 6. Professional independence

Attorney is solely responsible for their own professional judgment, advertising and solicitation compliance, and malpractice insurance. Nothing here makes Matra a law firm, an employer or a supervisor of Attorney.

## 7. Term and removal

Either party may end this on written notice. Attorney's obligations about records already received continue.
