# Name and logo policy (draft)

> **Draft. Not reviewed by a lawyer.** Trademark rights come from use and registration. Ask counsel or the fiscal sponsor whether to register the mark. Until the non-profit exists, the interim steward holds it in trust for the mission in [GOVERNANCE.md](../../GOVERNANCE.md).

The code is free to copy, modify and run under the [AGPL](../../LICENSE). The name **Matra** and the project's logo are not part of that license. The reason is safety, not control: a survivor who finds a fake "Matra" run by an abuser, a data harvester or a scam could be seriously harmed.

## You may, without asking

- Say your software is "based on Matra" or "a fork of Matra," as long as it is true and not misleading.
- Refer to the project by name when discussing it, linking to it, reviewing it or teaching it.
- Run an **unmodified** release under the Matra name for a pilot with a signed [pilot partner agreement](pilot-partner-agreement.md), or for private testing with synthetic data.

## You need written permission

- To offer a **modified** version under the Matra name or logo. Otherwise, rename it.
- To present a deployment as official, endorsed or operated by the project.
- To use the name in a company, product, domain or app-store listing name.

## Never

- Deploy under the name any version that adds tracking, advertising, data sale, or changes that weaken encryption or hide what the operator can see.
- Imply the project, its advisors or its partners endorse you.
- Use the name to collect data from, or direct, survivors to something we haven't approved.

## Enforcement

We will ask first and give notice. Our aim is to protect survivors, not to stop legitimate forks. If you want to fork and keep a mission-aligned approach, tell us and choose another name. We'll gladly link to you.
