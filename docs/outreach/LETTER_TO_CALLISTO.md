**Subject:** Thank you, from a small project you inspired

Dear Callisto board and team,

I'm writing to say thank you. For more than a decade, Callisto showed that survivors deserve private, survivor-controlled ways to record what happened and to learn they are not alone. Your published research, especially the 2018 matching design, is the foundation of a small nonprofit project I've been building, Matra, an open-source prototype for adult survivors in New Jersey and New York. It does not yet hold real survivor data.

I was very sorry to read the board's announcement in July. I know a closure is painful for the team and for the survivors who trusted you.

If it would ever be useful, I would be grateful to listen and learn: what you wish you had known earlier about funding, trust, and sustaining this work. I'll take whatever you're willing to share, including nothing at all.

Separately, and only if it would help: you mentioned looking for a new home for your technology. If any part of your mission, such as survivors who want somewhere to continue, could ever be supported by a project like ours, I'd be glad to talk on your terms and your timeline. That would always be within your own process, and with each survivor's own choice. There is no expectation and no need to reply.

Thank you for what you built and for the people you helped.

With gratitude and respect,
Aryan Malik
Founder, Matra
matra.reports@gmail.com · https://github.com/CANTSOAR/matra
