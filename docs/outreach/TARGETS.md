## A. NJ county sexual-violence programs (advocate reviewers; pilot partners)

The 21 county programs provide Confidential Sexual Violence Advocates (CSVAs). They are **campus-independent** and serve anyone regardless of school or reporting status, which makes them the natural reviewers and pilot channel. **Oversight:** the NJ DCF Division on Women administers sexual-violence funding to county providers ([DCF DOW SV services](https://www.nj.gov/dcf/divisions-offices/dow/sv-services.shtml)). OAG VOCA funds also pay for the CSVA programs ([FFY21 CSVA notice](https://www.nj.gov/oag/grants/2023-0209_FFY21-VOCA-Confidential-Sexual-Violence-Advocate-and-DCJ-Non-Competitive-Programs_NOAF.pdf)). Whether VIVA administers CSVA today is **UNVERIFIED**. The statewide hotline, **1-800-601-7200**, is current ([NJCASA Find Help](https://njcasa.org/find-help/)).
