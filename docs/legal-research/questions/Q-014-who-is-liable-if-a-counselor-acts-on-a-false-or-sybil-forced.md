## Question

If fake accounts force a match and the counselor contacts a real survivor (or the named person learns of it), what liability do Matra, the counselor and the partner organization face, and what review protocol reduces it?

## Context

Threshold is 2 distinct accounts. Mitigations today: 72 h release delay, Sybil signals shown to the counselor, human plausibility review before contact, a match is a lead not evidence. Background: [SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md §4a](../../SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md), [threat-model.md](../../threat-model.md) W13.

Interview 2026-11-10: under the bundle-and-hand-off model, the attorney who receives the case may act without ever speaking to the reporters (Q-021), which raises the stakes of a false or Sybil bundle.
