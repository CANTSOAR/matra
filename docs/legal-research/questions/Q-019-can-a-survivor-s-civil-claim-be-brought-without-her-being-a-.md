## Question

In NJ and NY, can a civil claim arising from a survivor's assault be brought (by her attorney, an organization, or anyone else) without her being a named party, being deposed, or testifying?

## Context

Founder's goal (interview 2026-11-10): "separate the survivor from the case" so that Matra can bundle her raw details and hand them to a lawyer who "takes the case from there" and "never has to question the original reporter", sparing her from reliving it. The founder asked whether any legal pathway ("or loophole") makes full insulation possible. This question asks what the minimum participation is on the civil side; Q-020 asks the same for criminal, Q-026 asks about pseudonyms as a partial measure.
