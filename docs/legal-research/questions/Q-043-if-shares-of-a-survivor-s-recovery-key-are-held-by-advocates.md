## Question

If a survivor's recovery key is split among trusted advocates or institutional key holders so she can recover a lost account, can those holders be compelled to combine their shares and decrypt her reports?

## Context

Today there is no password reset; a forgotten passphrase means the data is gone ([SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md](../../SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md)). The roadmap proposes 2-of-3 Shamir recovery among the survivor and trusted advocates (Callisto's approach: IACR ePrint 2024/962). Founder (interview 2026-11-11) asked for a recommendation; proposed: a recovery code only she holds by default, with share-based recovery optional and opt-in after a plain warning.
