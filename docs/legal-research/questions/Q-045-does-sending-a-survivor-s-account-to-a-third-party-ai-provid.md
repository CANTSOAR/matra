## Question

If Matra sends a survivor's account in plaintext to a third-party AI provider to help structure it, does that waive any privilege, count as a disclosure of personally identifying information under VAWA (34 U.S.C. § 12291(b)(2)) if grant-funded, and make the provider's copies or logs reachable by subpoena?

## Context

Founder (interview 2026-11-11): survivors "dump their report in a structured way, either through a guided walkthrough or by using AI to help them structure their thoughts". Today reports are end-to-end encrypted and Matra never holds plaintext ([SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md](../../SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md)); a hosted AI model would see plaintext unless the model runs on her device. Which model and provider is not decided.

Founder (2026-11-11): AI is deferred until after launch; "there is a very very very fine line if we decide to use it".
