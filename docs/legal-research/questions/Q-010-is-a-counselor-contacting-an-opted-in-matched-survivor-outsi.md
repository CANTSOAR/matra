## Question

When a survivor opted in at lodging time to be contacted if a match occurs, is the Legal Options Counselor's contact outside NJ RPC 7.3 and NY Rule 7.3, and what consent and contact protocol (including RPC 1.8(f) third-party-payer consent) makes that clear?

## Context

The counselor is a licensed attorney paid a fixed amount by Matra or a partner, offers options counseling only (RPC 1.2(c)), and we propose barring them from taking paid representation of anyone they contact. Contact happens only by the method she chose. Background: [LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md §7A](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#7a-legal-options-counselor-model-privilege-and-solicitation), [SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md §4a](../../SECURITY_WHITEPAPER.md).

Interview 2026-11-10: the founder proposes that the counselor be the lawyer who takes the case ("the counselor should probably be the lawyer"). That would remove the current bar on the counselor taking paid representation of survivors they contact, which the §7A argument against RPC 7.3 relies on.

Decided 2026-11-11 (founder): two roles, both attorneys. The counselor reviews matched reports, judges whether a case is strong enough, contacts each survivor, takes no fee and never takes the case. The litigating lawyer is chosen by the survivors, possibly from a short list the counselor offers. This replaces the merged role floated earlier the same day.
