## Question

If universities and nonprofits outside Matra hold n-of-m votes that are required to deploy any change to Matra's servers, could they be treated as joint venturers or co-operators liable for Matra's breaches or harms, and may a nonprofit's board lawfully give outsiders that veto over its operations?

## Context

Founder (interview 2026-11-11): "admin access should be a voting right, and people must have n of m votes to push a change, same as n of m keys." Holders are the same universities and nonprofits proposed as key holders (Q-016). Practical note: an emergency security fix would also need n votes.
