## Question

Given how Matra lists verified attorneys and runs a Legal Options Counselor program, is it a lawyer directory, a nonprofit lawyer referral service, or a legal service plan under NJ RPC 7.3(e), and must it register?

## Context

Survivors choose an attorney themselves from a list of verified partners; Matra charges no fee to anyone and takes no share of fees. Separately, a matched group of survivors is opened by a Legal Options Counselor (a licensed attorney paid by Matra or a partner, not by survivors). The planned matcher would assign each matched group to one counselor by jurisdiction and circumstance (see Q-017). Background: [LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md §5](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#5-unauthorized-practice-of-law-lawyer-referral-and-fee-sharing), [LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md §7A](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#7a-legal-options-counselor-model-privilege-and-solicitation).

Interview 2026-11-10: the founder wants a network of lawyers treated "as a distributed system that we send cases to based on demand". How network lawyers are paid, and whether Matra receives anything, is not yet decided.

Proposed 2026-11-11 (founder chose 'whatever protects Matra most'; pending counsel): Matra receives nothing from any lawyer or case, ever. The counselor is paid a flat amount by Matra or a partner. The litigating lawyer's fee is between that lawyer and the survivors (e.g. contingency), with no involvement by Matra. Launch states: CA, NJ, NY, PA (see Q-034).

Interview 2026-11-11: the founder asked whether a JDoe-style model is sustainable. The memo ([§10](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#10-competitive-landscape-verified-as-of-2026-10-06)) reports JDoe was funded by attorneys paying for access to potential claims [VERIFY], which it says is impermissible in NJ. Proposed: no money from lawyers to Matra (see above).

Founder (2026-11-11) recalls JDoe paying lawyers out of the case rather than lawyers paying JDoe. Either direction ties Matra's money to cases; a nonlawyer taking part of a fee is fee sharing (RPC 5.4(a)) [VERIFY JDoe's actual model].
