## Question

Can one attorney receive and act on a bundle combining several survivors' reports about the same person, and jointly represent them, under RPC 1.7 and 1.8(g)?

## Context

Founder (interview 2026-11-10): the matcher takes N reports, combines them into one case, and assigns it to one lawyer. Survivors' accounts may diverge, which is where joint-representation conflicts usually arise. [LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md §7A](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#7a-legal-options-counselor-model-privilege-and-solicitation) item 5 currently assumes the counselor does not represent two matched survivors jointly.

Founder (2026-11-10, follow-up): the lawyer's client is everyone who reported the same named person, i.e. joint representation is the intended model.
