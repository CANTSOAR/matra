## Question

Can an attorney who receives a bundle of Matra reports accept and act on the matter (investigate, send demands, file) without ever communicating with the survivors who wrote them, consistent with RPC 1.2, 1.4 and the reasonable-inquiry duty of R. 1:4-8 / FRCP 11?

## Context

Founder's proposed flow (interview 2026-11-10): once a threshold is met, Matra combines N reports into a "case" and assigns it to a lawyer in Matra's network; the lawyer takes it from there and never questions the original reporters. Open fact: who the lawyer's client would be (each survivor, none, Matra). This differs from the current counselor model, where the counselor contacts each matched survivor separately ([LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md §7A](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#7a-legal-options-counselor-model-privilege-and-solicitation)).

Founder (2026-11-10, follow-up): the client is "everyone on the other side of one bad guy", i.e. all survivors who reported the same named person. The founder now thinks the counselor should be the lawyer, and if full insulation is impossible a lawyer is needed anyway.
