## Question

Can Matra, a nonlawyer organization not employed or supervised by the receiving attorney, prepare a case file with recommended legal theories and arguments for that attorney without engaging in the unauthorized practice of law (and what RPC 5.3 supervision would make it permissible)?

## Context

Founder (interview 2026-11-10): the lawyer should get the case "on a silver platter with recommended arguments and everything" ("the classic manila folder with all the details the lawyer might need"); the lawyer "applies their legal double checks and then bangs it out". The file combines the survivors' reports and OSINT on the named person (Q-029). Background: [LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md §5](../../LEGAL_RESEARCH_MEMO.md#5-unauthorized-practice-of-law-lawyer-referral-and-fee-sharing) (scrivener work is low risk; advising on charges and elements is not).

Proposed 2026-11-11: the file Matra hands over contains the survivors' own reports, structured, with no legal theories or recommended arguments; the counselor and litigating lawyer supply the legal analysis.
