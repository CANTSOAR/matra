# Advisory Outreach: Organizational Contacts

Org-level landing and contact pages only (no individual personal emails). Verified reachable 2026-10-06. Use the organizations' general contact forms and phone lines. Find named faculty and staff through official directory pages when you are ready to reach out.

## New Jersey survivor coalitions

- **NJ Coalition Against Sexual Assault (NJCASA)**: https://njcasa.org/ | contact: https://njcasa.org/about/contact/ | Statewide advocacy and capacity-building coalition representing about 30 county sexual-violence programs. **This is the most relevant NJ coalition for a sexual-assault intake product** (start here). 24-hour statewide sexual violence hotline: 800-601-7200.
- **NJ Coalition to End Domestic Violence (NJCEDV)**: https://njcedv.org/ | contact: https://njcedv.org/contact/ | Statewide domestic-violence coalition (policy, training, member DV programs). Relevant for intimate-partner sexual violence and DV overlap. Statewide DV hotline: 1-800-572-SAFE (7233).

## Law school clinics (NJ / NY)

- **Rutgers Law School: Clinics index**: https://law.rutgers.edu/professional-skills/clinics | More than 18 clinics across the Newark and Camden campuses.
  - **Domestic Violence Clinic (Camden)**: https://law.rutgers.edu/professional-skills/clinics/domestic-violence-clinic | Represents DV victims (e.g., final restraining order hearings) and runs a statewide legal-information helpline for survivors. Clinic phone: (856) 225-6425.
  - **RBG Women's Rights and Gender Justice Clinic (Newark)**: https://law.rutgers.edu/professional-skills/clinics/womens-rights-and-gender-justice-clinic | Revives and expands the Women's Rights Litigation Clinic that Ruth Bader Ginsburg founded at Rutgers-Newark in the early 1970s. Likely the best fit for evidentiary and survivor-rights review.
  - **Legislative & Policy Advocacy Clinic**: https://law.rutgers.edu/professional-skills/clinics/legislative-and-policy-advocacy-clinic | Possible fit for NJ statutory work (VCCO, privilege).
- **Seton Hall Law: Center for Social Justice (Newark)**: https://law.shu.edu/center-social-justice/ | Seton Hall's clinical hub. Its Family Law clinic handles restraining orders, custody and support, and it also runs Immigrants' Rights/International Human Rights (including trafficking) and Criminal Defense clinics. General line: 973-642-8700; 833 McCarter Hwy, Newark, NJ 07102.
- **NYU School of Law: Clinics**: https://www.law.nyu.edu/academics/clinics (by topic: https://www.law.nyu.edu/academics/clinics/clinics-by-topic) | More than 35 clinics. Most relevant: **Technology Law & Policy Clinic** (https://www.law.nyu.edu/academics/clinics/semester/technologylawandpolicy; privacy and consumer rights), Family Defense Clinic, and Civil Rights in the Criminal Legal System Clinic. NYU has no dedicated sexual-assault or DV clinic listed. Clinical Law Center office: (212) 998-6430.

## Training and research bodies (trauma-informed interviewing)

- **End Violence Against Women International (EVAWI)**: https://evawintl.org/ | contact: https://evawintl.org/contact-us/ | Nonprofit training organization for law enforcement, prosecutors and advocates on sexual-assault response. It publishes training bulletins and runs the OnLine Training Institute (OLTI) and Start by Believing. General: 509-684-9800.
- **Forensic Experiential Trauma Interview (FETI)**: https://www.certifiedfeti.com/ | "Certified FETI" describes itself as the founding and governing body of FETI. It offers methodology courses and certification. Contact is through the site's contact form. FETI was developed by **Russell Strand** (formerly U.S. Army Military Police School, Behavioral Sciences Education & Training Division; see https://www.army.mil/article/130936/). The site does not name him on its homepage, so confirm his current affiliation before outreach.
